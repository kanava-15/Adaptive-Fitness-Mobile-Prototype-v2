Goblet Squat

A fundamental lower-body strength movement. Holding the weight anteriorly encourages an upright torso, greater depth, and massive core engagement.

Setup & Execution

fitness_center Kettlebell Intermediate Bilateral

Target Anatomy

Quadriceps Glutes Core (Stabilizer)

warning Precautions

Ensure your knees track over your toes and do not cave inward. Keep your chest tall to prevent your lower back from rounding at the bottom of the squat.

Step-by-Step

1

Hold the kettlebell by the horns close to your chest.

2

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes pointed slightly outward.

3

Brace your core and lower your hips down and back, keeping your chest up.

4

Drive through your mid-foot to return to the starting position.

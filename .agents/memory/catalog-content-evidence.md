CARs references are evidence for individual motion descriptions, not proof of clinical benefit or FRC/Kinstretch certification. The Twin Cities Movement morning guide has mismatched text in its scapula/elbow and fingers/toes sections; do not use those sections to add drills.

**Why:** Direct inspection found copied spine and wrist text under unrelated joints. Quota-driven use would manufacture unsupported identities.

**How to apply:** Inspect the actual section for every new joint drill; omit mismatched evidence rather than extrapolating a drill from a heading.

Ashtanga series names and memberships must remain lineage-qualified. Philippa Asher's charts describe what she learned from Pattabhi Jois and Sharath Jois and note differing Vasisthasana/Vishvamitrasana labels.

**Why:** The references explicitly acknowledge naming differences; claiming universal fixed naming or progression would exceed the evidence.

**How to apply:** Maintain original descriptive English labels and qualified lineage context; do not auto-progress later-series yoga or treat a source check as expert certification.
